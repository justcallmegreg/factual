# Phrasebook: avoid and use

The draft reads as a request to understand what happened, not as a counter-attack.

| Avoid | Use instead |
| --- | --- |
| "You never told me." | "I cannot find a message that tells me about this change." |
| "You agreed to this." | "On Thu 29 Oct, 13:40 CET, you wrote: …" |
| "That is not true." | "The records show something different. This is what I have." |
| "Obviously", "clearly", "as I said" | Leave it out |
| "You changed your mind." | "Your message of 29 Oct says 5 February. Your message of 18 Nov says 12 February." |
| "Why did you hide this?" | "Can you help me find where this was shared?" |
| "This is unacceptable." | "I want to find a solution that works for both of us." |

## Banned words and phrases

The checker rejects a draft that contains any of these outside a quote:

- Insults, sarcasm, threats and ultimatums: stupid, ridiculous, pathetic, or else, last warning, you had better
- Blame words: your fault, you failed, you ignored, you hide, you hid, hidden, lie, lied, liar, dishonest, fraud
- Legal labels: breach, bad faith, negligence, liable, liability, damages, lawyer, sue, legal action
- Absolute words: always, never, every time, nobody, everyone knows
- Dismissals: obviously, clearly, as I said, as I told you, that is not true, not true, you agreed to this, you changed your mind, unacceptable
