```text
BRIEF

             A2 <one line> — Contradicted

Evidence     E41233 Verified · seal 482 · /proof E41233

DRAFT (edit it, then send it yourself)
<wish to understand; possible misalignment>
What the records show:
- <date, time> · <origin> · [[quote:E]] (E)
What I cannot find:
- <missing record> (<scope>)
Questions:
- <open question>
<proposal to agree in writing; evidence pack attached>
```

Verdicts for assertions: Supported, Contradicted, Superseded (acknowledged or not acknowledged),
Not on record. Every bullet under "What the records show" cites an entry. Every bullet under
"What I cannot find" ends with its scope in parentheses: the dates and terms you searched.
